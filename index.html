About Me

I'm a Design Engineering student at Imperial College London who has just finished second year, interested in developing physical products through CAD, simulation, and hands-on prototyping. I enjoy taking ideas from early concept through detailed design, 3D printing, testing, and refinement.

My approach combines technical development with an understanding of how people actually interact with products, using user feedback, ergonomics, and human-centred thinking to get to outcomes that are functional and genuinely well considered.
